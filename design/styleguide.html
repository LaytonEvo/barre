The design system for the Barre By Kelly site and members' platform. Every colour pair below was checked against WCAG 2.2 with a script, not estimated. Nothing here is extracted from Kelly's real brand yet, because /brand was empty — this is the fallback direction, built in two layers so swapping in her palette is a one-file change.

What the tokens guarantee

Accessibility

  • Focus is restyled, never removed — 2px plum outline, 2px offset, set globally in tokens.css.
  • prefers-reduced-motion collapses every duration token to 1ms, so no component has to remember.
  • Tap targets 44px minimum, booking CTAs 48px.
  • Body text never below 16px, which also stops iOS zooming form fields on focus.
  • Status always carries text as well as colour.

Still missing

  • Kelly's logo and photos. Until they arrive, images are flat token-coloured blocks at the right aspect ratio. No stock photography will be added.
  • The town. Every local-SEO decision depends on it.
  • Class names, times, venues, prices. Shown as TODO above rather than guessed.
  • Tone notes — phrases Kelly uses, colours she dislikes.
